What gets passed on

Families often pass on their wealth and business, but not always what built them.

Many families spend decades building wealth and businesses for the next generation. The greatest risk is often not losing the assets. It is failing to pass on the faith, principles, character and wisdom that created them.

What the research says

Wealth rarely fails on paper. It fails in the family.

Decades of research point the same way: the plans are usually sound. What breaks down is trust, communication and preparation of the next generation.

70%of wealth transitions fail
The Williams study

Why family wealth transitions fail

Seven in ten family wealth transitions failed. The cause was rarely the plan.

  • 60%Breakdown of communication and trust within the family
  • 25%Heirs not prepared for the responsibility
  • 15%Everything else: tax, legal and investment
Williams and Preisser, Preparing Heirs (2003). 3,250 families over 20 years
