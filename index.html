What gets passed on

Families often pass on their wealth and business, but not always what built them.

Many families spend decades building wealth and businesses for the next generation. The greatest risk is often not losing the assets. It is failing to pass on the faith, principles, character and wisdom that created them.

Do we have to commit to the whole journey?

No. Each stage is agreed before it begins, and you may stop at any point.

What does it cost?

It depends on your family, the people taking part and what you choose to include. Every journey is a custom quote, prepared after your Legacy Snapshot and paid in stages, before each piece of work.

We already have advisers.

Good. We sit beside them, never in their place. Your lawyers, accountants and wealth advisers keep their mandates and join the work where you wish.

Is this financial advice?

No. We do not manage client assets, sell third-party financial products, or receive commissions or referral payments. Entruvant does not provide investment, legal or tax advice.

What if our children will not engage?

That is common, and it is why each person is heard in confidence and given a real responsibility to hold, not a lecture.

Is it only for Christian families?

Entruvant is built on Christian faith. The journey is offered with a faith dimension or in a secular form, according to your wishes.

Do we begin with the family or the business?

Either, or both. Some begin with the business and bring the family in later. Others begin with the family. Your Legacy Snapshot shows where to start.

Where do you work?

Wherever your family is. We meet in person or online, and Cornerstone is held at a location chosen with you.
